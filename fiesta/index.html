Modules

Everything a small business needs every day.

Fiesta's navigation is organized just like this: documents for daily business, master data for items and customers, classifications for groups and terms. Every form switches between overview and master data view with one click.

Documents

  • RequestsRecord customer requests and follow them up
  • QuotationsTurn a request into a quotation with line items
  • InvoicesLines, document discount, VAT, PDF output and EDI
  • Credit notesBook corrections cleanly against the invoice

Master data

  • ItemsList price, 10 price groups with validity dates, unit of measure and weight
  • CustomersCompany or private person, address, contact details and customer group

Classifications

  • Item groupsStructure your range, for example Hardware
  • Customer groupsEnd customers, resellers and their own price groups
  • Payment termsSet up payment terms once, use them everywhere
  • Delivery termsShipping conditions per customer or document

System

  • Recycle binRestore deleted documents and master data
  • Custom fieldsExtend forms with your own fields
  • AdministrationUsers & permissions, companies, log, backup
  • DB connectorChoose and connect your database
  • Export & importMove data in and out from any form

Sample invoice

From line item to finished invoice.

Fiesta calculates as you type. Prices come from the customer's price group, and discounts and VAT appear instantly.

  1. Pick the customer

    Address and price group are taken from master data.

  2. Add items

    Choose the item number and enter a quantity. Fiesta fills in the description, unit and unit price.

  3. Apply a discount

    Per line, or as a document discount on the whole invoice.

  4. Save and create the PDF

    Every change is logged with user and timestamp.
